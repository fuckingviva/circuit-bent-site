Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This page explains what happens to your data when you visit circuitbent.store or buy CIRCUIT BENT. Short version: we collect almost nothing ourselves, and purchases are handled entirely by Gumroad.

Data controller: Adrian Castillo, sole proprietor, trading as CIRCUIT BENT.
Contact: hello@circuitbent.store

What we collect

This site is static — no analytics, no cookies, no tracking scripts, no contact form, no mailing list, no embedded third-party widgets. The only thing it sends anywhere is whatever your browser and our web host exchange to load the page (standard server access logs, e.g. IP address and request time), which we don't use to identify you and don't look at.

If you email us at hello@circuitbent.store, we receive whatever you send — your email address and the contents of your message. We use this only to reply to you, and don't share it anywhere.

Buying CIRCUIT BENT

The "Buy" button on this site is a plain link to our Gumroad listing (thoughtful65.gumroad.com/l/circuit-bent) — it's not embedded, so when you click it you leave our site and the entire purchase (payment details, email collection, VAT/tax, receipt, license key delivery) happens on Gumroad's own platform, under their own privacy policy:

  • gumroad.com/privacy

We never see or store your card details. Gumroad does pass us your email address and purchase details (via their seller dashboard/webhook) so we can support your purchase if you contact us — that's the only personal data about a purchase that reaches us directly.

Your license key

Your license key is generated from the email address you purchased with. Verifying it happens entirely offline, on your own computer — the plugin does not phone home, track usage, or send telemetry of any kind.

Your rights (GDPR/UK GDPR)

If you're in the EU/EEA/UK, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete the data we hold about you (mainly: email correspondence, and purchase records Gumroad shares with us), object to how we use it, or lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Email hello@circuitbent.store for any of this — we'll respond within a month. For anything related to your payment itself, Gumroad is the right place to ask, since they hold that data.

Changes

If this changes — say, if we ever add analytics or a mailing list — we'll update this page and the date above.
